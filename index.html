RESEARCH

Neural Model Checking

What I work on, in two paragraphs.

My PhD work develops neural model checking, a framework for temporal-logic model checking that uses neural networks as formal proof certificates for both safety and liveness. Certificates are learned from executions of the system and then validated by symbolic checking based on satisfiability solving. An accepted certificate is therefore a sound proof of correctness.

Checking a candidate certificate is often much easier than synthesising one. We design neural architectures that can be trained by constraint solvers, making the learning procedure unsupervised, formally sound, and practically effective. On textbook-level hardware designs with rich linear-temporal-logic specifications, the approach verifies liveness and combined safety-liveness properties with orders-of-magnitude speedups over strong model-checking baselines.
